The tree, with detail

Same five stages, redrawn as an illustration rather than a diagram: scalloped foliage in three greens with a lit side and a shaded underside, a tapered trunk with bark and roots, real ground, and at the finished stage fruit and a bird. Still one ink outline weight, still flat fills, still a single SVG.

  • Foliage is one scalloped clump shape reused fourteen times in three greens, back to front: deep shadow low and centre, mid tone, light tint up and to the left where the light is. Loose leaves at the edges break the silhouette.
  • Trunk tapers, has a shaded side and two bark lines, splits into three branches you can see through gaps in the canopy, and flares into roots.
  • Ground is a mound with a darker soil ring, grass tufts and two pebbles.
  • Fruit is butter with a stem and leaf, one per completed ritual, only on the finished tree.
  • Bird lands on the finished tree. Cheap to add, big payoff for a kid opening the app after the last tap.
  • Backplate is a faint pale-green disc so the tree sits in a space rather than on the card.
